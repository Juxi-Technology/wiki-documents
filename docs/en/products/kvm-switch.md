## Overview

The 4-in-1 KVM switch combines HDMI switching, TTL serial, Bluetooth, and USB expansion for robotics development and debugging. One-click switching between arm hosts/displays/peripherals.

**Key features**:

- HDMI multi-device switching
- TTL serial (UART) debugging
- Bluetooth wireless
- USB hub expansion
