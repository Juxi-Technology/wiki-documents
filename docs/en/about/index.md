# About Us

## Company Overview

**Juxi Technology** is headquartered in **Qianhai, Shenzhen**, specializing in **intelligent hardware and embodied intelligence**. Our core technology covers **multimodal perception, edge AI, and robotics control**, delivering localized solutions to global markets through open-source collaboration.

Following our philosophy of **"Leverage HK, serve mainland, go global"**, we bring open-source robotics and edge AI from Shenzhen to developers worldwide.

> **Building the Future of Open-Source Robotics** — we believe the future of robotics belongs to open ecosystems.

## Core Technology

| Domain | Content |
|--------|---------|
| **Multimodal Perception** | Vision, voice, IMU and multi-sensor fusion |
| **Edge AI** | NVIDIA Jetson platform, PyTorch/TensorRT optimization |
| **Robotics Control** | Robot arms, dexterous hands, servos and motion control |

## Open-Source Commitment

**Every JuxiTech product is open source.** We publicly publish:

- 📐 Full hardware schematics
- 🔧 Firmware source code
- 🖥️ CAD files
- 📦 SDK code

All freely available on our [Feishu Wiki](https://juxitech.feishu.cn) and [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/Juxi-Technology). Our community of developers and researchers continuously contributes to and improves this platform.

## Ecosystem Support

Out-of-the-box support for:

- 🤖 **ROS 2**
- 🧠 **LeRobot** (HuggingFace)
- 🖥️ **NVIDIA Jetson**
- 🍓 **Raspberry Pi**

## Product Lineup

- **Robot Arms**: open-source 6-axis arms for research, education, and industrial automation
- **Dexterous Hands**: multi-finger hands for embodied AI and human-robot interaction research
- **Edge Computing**: NVIDIA Jetson-powered dev kits with PyTorch and TensorRT optimization
- **Sensors & IMU**: high-precision inertial navigation and attitude sensing for autonomous systems
