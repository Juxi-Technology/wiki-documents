# Why We Build Open — The Case for Open-Source Robotics Hardware

At JuxiTech, every product we ship includes full hardware schematics, firmware source code, and CAD files — freely available on this Wiki. In an industry where proprietary lock-in is the norm, why do we build everything open?

## The Real Cost of Closed Hardware

When a research lab buys a proprietary robotic arm, they're not just buying hardware — they're buying into a closed ecosystem. Custom controllers, proprietary communication protocols, and black-box firmware make it impossible to modify, repair, or truly understand the system. When something breaks, you ship it back. When you need it to do something the manufacturer didn't anticipate, you're stuck. For researchers pushing the boundaries of what's possible, closed hardware is a ceiling.

## Open Source as a Research Accelerator

We've seen what happens when you open the black box. The SO-ARM101 has been modified by university labs for specific grasping tasks. The AmazingHand has been adapted for prosthetics research. Our Jetson edge AI platforms have been integrated into autonomous robots we never imagined. Each modification contributes back to the community, creating a virtuous cycle of improvement that no single company could achieve alone.

## Built in Shenzhen, Used Worldwide

Our Qianhai location puts us at the center of global hardware manufacturing. We leverage Shenzhen's supply chain to keep costs low while maintaining production quality. The result: open-source robotics hardware that's accessible to labs, schools, and makers worldwide — not just well-funded institutions.

## What's Next

We're expanding our product line with more embodied intelligence platforms, physical AI tools, and edge computing solutions. All open. All documented. All built for the community. Follow our Wiki and blog for upcoming releases, technical deep-dives, and community showcases.
