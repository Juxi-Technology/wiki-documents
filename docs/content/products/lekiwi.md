## Overview

Lekiwi is Juxi Technology's low-cost embodied intelligence mobile robot with intuitive servo control — ideal for classroom teaching, beginner learning, and maker projects. Optional 12V power (5521 DC).

**Key features**:

- Simple structure, low cost, batch deployable
- Intuitive servo control
- Shared LeRobot ecosystem with SO-ARM101
