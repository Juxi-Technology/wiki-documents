## Install the Chassis Car

1、Place the chassis car directly under the cart, and note the installation direction of the chassis car **(see the “XLerobot” lettering on the chassis)**

The installation positions of the camera tower base and the chassis car are shown in the figure

![Image 5](../../../../public/images/tutorials/robot-arms/xlerobot/03-Assembly-Assembled-Kit/5.png)

2、Pass the **90CM servo extension cable** through the metal mesh of the cart

![Image 6](../../../../public/images/tutorials/robot-arms/xlerobot/03-Assembly-Assembled-Kit/6.png)

3、Use 6 **M3\*16 hex screws** and 6 **M3 nuts** to fix the chassis car to the bottom of the cart

![Image 7](../../../../public/images/tutorials/robot-arms/xlerobot/03-Assembly-Assembled-Kit/7.png)

## Install the Camera Tower Base

Note the **grooves** pointed to by the two arrows, and clip them onto the edge of the bottom layer of the cart
