# Module Firmware Flashing

## How to Flash the Module Firmware

## 1. Voice Chip Flashing Steps

## 1.1 Device Connection

![Image 1](../../../../public/images/tutorials/accessories/ai-voice-module/Firmware-Flashing/1.png)

## 1.2. Open the Flashing Software

Open the "Voice Chip Firmware Flashing Tool" folder in the attachments and click ”PACK_UPDATE_TOOL.exe“

![Image 2](../../../../public/images/tutorials/accessories/ai-voice-module/Firmware-Flashing/2.png)

Select the CI1302 chip, then click “Firmware Upgrade”

![Image 3](../../../../public/images/tutorials/accessories/ai-voice-module/Firmware-Flashing/3.png)

Enter the firmware upgrade interface; press "F1" to view the help

Click to select the firmware you just created, and locate the factory firmware “CI1302中文单麦_V00681_UART1_115200_2M” in the attachments.

![Image 4](../../../../public/images/tutorials/accessories/ai-voice-module/Firmware-Flashing/4.png)

Select the firmware, find the corresponding serial port number, and click the checkbox to tick it

![Image 5](../../../../public/images/tutorials/accessories/ai-voice-module/Firmware-Flashing/5.png)

Move the slide switch to the Download position; Download means flashing firmware to the voice chip (that is, the right-hand side)

![Image 6](../../../../public/images/tutorials/accessories/ai-voice-module/Firmware-Flashing/6.png)

After switching to the corresponding position, press the RST button on the voice interaction module to enter flashing mode; wait for flashing to succeed, then click “Exit”.

![Image 7](../../../../public/images/tutorials/accessories/ai-voice-module/Firmware-Flashing/7.png)
